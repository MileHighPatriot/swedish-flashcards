### Grammar – Past tense & word order (rule cards)
rule | How do you form the past tense of an -ar verb? | Drop the final -a and add -ade: arbeta → arbetade. | Same form for every person: jag, du, hon, vi, de arbetade. | past-tense, -ade, lesson-01
rule | Does the past tense verb change with the person (jag, hon, vi)? | No. One form for everyone: jag arbetade, hon arbetade, vi arbetade. | Unlike English, no extra endings for the person. | past-tense, lesson-01
rule | What word order do you use when a sentence starts with a time word like igår? | Verb second, subject third: Igår arbetade jag. | Verb second rule: whatever goes first, the verb takes seat 2. | word-order, verb-second, lesson-01
rule | Which stem endings take -te in the past tense? | k, p, t, s, x (and ck counts as k): köp→köpte, läs→läste, åk→åkte. | These are hard sounds made without your voice, so a hard t follows them. | past-tense, -te, lesson-02
rule | What ending do the other stems take in the past tense? | -de: ring→ringde, hör→hörde, lev→levde, bygg→byggde. | Check the last sound of the stem after dropping -a. | past-tense, -de, lesson-02
rule | What is the past tense of bo and tro? | bodde and trodde (double d). | Verbs ending in a vowel double the d. | past-tense, -de, lesson-02
rule | What is the past tense of känna (to feel, to know)? | kände | It loses one n. | past-tense, -de, lesson-02
rule | How do you make a yes/no question in Swedish? | Put the verb first: Köpte du boken? | No extra word like "did". | questions, lesson-02
rule | Where does the verb go after a question word (vad, var, när, hur)? | Seat 2: Vad köpte du? Var bodde du? | Same verb-second rule. | questions, verb-second, lesson-02
rule | What form of the verb follows kan, vill, ska, måste, får? | The infinitive: Jag kan prata svenska. | Never add -r after a modal. | modals, infinitive
rule | What does "så att" do? | It gives a purpose, "so that": Jag lär mig svenska så att jag kan prata med min svärmor. | Don't drop "att". | connectors
rule | What does "som" do in "en vän som bor i Sverige"? | It replaces the subject. Don't add han/hon after it. | Relative clause. | connectors
rule | Where does "inte" go in a subordinate clause (after att, som, om)? | Before the verb: Jag vet att han inte kommer. | In main clauses it comes after the verb: Han kommer inte. | negation, word-order
rule | What is the difference between hem and hemma? | hem = direction (going home): Jag går hem. hemma = location (at home): Jag är hemma. | Movement vs place. | vocabulary, grammar
rule | What is the plural of "barn" and how do you say "my children"? | barn (unchanged), mina barn. | Plural possessive is mina. | plural
rule | When do you capitalize in Swedish? | Only the first word of a sentence and names. Days, months, and languages are lowercase: lördag, november, svenska. | Don't capitalize mid-sentence: "Jag lagade middag", not "Jag Lagade". | spelling
rule | How is k pronounced before ö, ä, e, i, y? | A soft "sh"-like sound: köpte ≈ SHERP-teh. | Stress the first syllable. ö is like "er" without the r. | pronunciation

### Grammar – My mistakes (fix-the-error cards, from my own work)
fix | Igår jag arbetade. | Igår arbetade jag. | Verb in seat 2 after a time word. | my-mistake, verb-second
fix | Igår jag arbeta. | Igår arbetade jag. | Past needs -ade and verb in seat 2. | my-mistake, past-tense, verb-second
fix | Igår arbetar jag. (yesterday) | Igår arbetade jag. | Present tense means now. Past needs -ade. | my-mistake, past-tense
fix | Vi städa igår. | Vi städade igår. | Add -ade for the past, not the infinitive. | my-mistake, past-tense
fix | Hon lagar mat igår. | Hon lagade mat igår. | Past tense for yesterday. | my-mistake, past-tense
fix | Jag köpedde en bok. | Jag köpte en bok. | Stem köp ends in p, so -te, not -de. | my-mistake, -te
fix | Jag kan pratar svenska. | Jag kan prata svenska. | Infinitive after a modal. | my-mistake, modals
fix | Jag lär mig svenska så jag kan prata. | Jag lär mig svenska så att jag kan prata. | så att gives a purpose. | my-mistake, connectors
fix | Jag har en vän som han bor i Sverige. | Jag har en vän som bor i Sverige. | som replaces the subject. | my-mistake, connectors
fix | min barnet | mina barn | Plural possessive is mina, and barn doesn't change. | my-mistake, plural
fix | Jag vet att han kommer inte. | Jag vet att han inte kommer. | inte goes before the verb in a subordinate clause. | my-mistake, negation
fix | Jag är hem. | Jag är hemma. | hemma = at home (location). | my-mistake, hem-hemma
fix | Jag går hemma. | Jag går hem. | hem = going home (direction). | my-mistake, hem-hemma
fix | Vi lyssnade på music. | Vi lyssnade på musik. | The Swedish spelling is musik. | my-mistake, spelling
fix | Vi lysnade på musik. | Vi lyssnade på musik. | Lyssna has a double s. | my-mistake, spelling, watch-spelling
fix | Ikväll var det en bra kväll. (about last night) | Igår kväll var det en bra kväll. | ikväll = tonight. Use igår kväll for last night. | my-mistake, time-words
fix | Igår kväll vart det en bra kväll. | Igår kväll var det en bra kväll. | var = was. vart = where to. | my-mistake, spelling
fix | Jag var off igår. | Jag var ledig igår. | "Off" isn't Swedish. ledig = off, free from work. | my-mistake, vocabulary
fix | Jag Lagade middag och Vi åt. | Jag lagade middag och vi åt. | No capitals mid-sentence. | my-mistake, spelling
fix | Jag alskar min svarmor. | Jag älskar min svärmor. | Always type å, ä, ö. | my-mistake, spelling
