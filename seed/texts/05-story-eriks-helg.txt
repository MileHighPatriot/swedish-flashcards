# Lesson 05 – Eriks helg
# The reading text from the Lesson 5 printable.
# Lines:  Swedish | English
Erik har en fru, Anna, och två barn. | Erik has a wife, Anna, and two children.
På lördag vill barnen leka i parken. | On Saturday the children want to play in the park.
Anna måste arbeta, så Erik ska vara hemma med barnen. | Anna has to work, so Erik is going to be home with the children.
Han kan inte laga middag, men han kan åka till affären och köpa mat. | He cannot make dinner, but he can go to the store and buy food.
På söndag ska de träffa Annas mamma. | On Sunday they are going to meet Anna's mother.
Barnen vill ge mormor en present. | The children want to give grandma a present.
Mormor säger att de måste komma tidigt. | Grandma says they have to come early.
