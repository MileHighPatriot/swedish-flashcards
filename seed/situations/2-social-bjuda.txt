# Bjuda hem en vän
# Category: Social
# English: Inviting a friend over
# Scene: You call a friend to invite them to dinner.
# Goal: Agree on a day and a time, and find out what they eat.
# Lines:  Speaker | Swedish | English
Du | Hej! Vad gör du på lördag? | Hi! What are you doing on Saturday?
Vän | Ingenting särskilt. Hur så? | Nothing special. Why?
Du | Vill du komma hem till oss på middag? | Would you like to come to our place for dinner?
Vän | Ja, gärna! Vilken tid? | Yes, gladly! What time?
Du | Klockan sex, om det passar. | Six o'clock, if that suits you.
Vän | Det passar bra. Ska jag ta med något? | That suits me well. Shall I bring anything?
Du | Nej, det behövs inte. Äter du allt? | No, there's no need. Do you eat everything?
Vän | Jag äter inte fisk, men allt annat går bra. | I don't eat fish, but everything else is fine.
Du | Inga problem. Då lagar jag kyckling. | No problem. Then I'll cook chicken.
Vän | Vad gott! Vi ses på lördag. | How tasty! See you on Saturday.
Du | Vi ses! | See you!
