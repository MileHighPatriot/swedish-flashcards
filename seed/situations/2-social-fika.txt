# Fika med en kollega
# Category: Social
# English: Coffee break with a coworker
# Scene: A coworker asks you to take a coffee break.
# Goal: Talk about the weekend and your plans for tonight.
# Lines:  Speaker | Swedish | English
Kollega | Ska vi ta en fika? | Shall we have a coffee break?
Du | Ja, gärna! Jag behöver en paus. | Yes, gladly! I need a break.
Kollega | Hur var din helg? | How was your weekend?
Du | Den var bra. Jag var hemma och vilade. Och din? | It was good. I was at home and rested. And yours?
Kollega | Jag var i stugan med familjen. | I was at the cabin with the family.
Du | Vad härligt! Var det fint väder? | How lovely! Was the weather nice?
Kollega | Ja, solen sken hela helgen. | Yes, the sun shone all weekend.
Du | Vad skönt. Vill du ha en bulle? | How nice. Do you want a bun?
Kollega | Ja tack! Vad ska du göra i kväll? | Yes please! What are you going to do tonight?
Du | Jag ska laga mat och titta på en film. | I'm going to cook and watch a film.
Kollega | Det låter mysigt. | That sounds cosy.
