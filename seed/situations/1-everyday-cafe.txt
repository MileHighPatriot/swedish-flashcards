# På kaféet
# Category: Everyday
# English: At the café
# Scene: You walk into a café in town.
# Goal: Order a coffee and a cinnamon bun, and pay by card.
# Lines:  Speaker | Swedish | English
Barista | Hej! Vad vill du ha? | Hi! What would you like?
Du | Hej! Jag vill ha en kaffe och en kanelbulle, tack. | Hi! I'd like a coffee and a cinnamon bun, please.
Barista | Vill du ha mjölk i kaffet? | Do you want milk in your coffee?
Du | Ja, lite mjölk, tack. | Yes, a little milk, please.
Barista | Ska du äta här eller ta med? | Are you eating here or taking it with you?
Du | Jag äter här. | I'm eating here.
Barista | Det blir sextiofem kronor. | That will be sixty-five kronor.
Du | Kan jag betala med kort? | Can I pay by card?
Barista | Ja, det går bra. Varsågod! | Yes, that's fine. Here you go!
Du | Tack så mycket! | Thank you very much!
Barista | Tack själv. Ha en bra dag! | Thank you. Have a good day!
Du | Tack, detsamma! | Thanks, you too!
